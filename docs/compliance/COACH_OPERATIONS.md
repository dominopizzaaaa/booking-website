## Responsibility boundary to approve

For the current product model, the recommended operating baseline is:

- the club is the service organiser and owns selection, safe recruitment,
  supervision, venue/activity risk, worker classification, and service insurance;
- each coach supplies truthful, current evidence and reports changes; and
- Courtly supplies workflow and records only where explicitly implemented, does
  not silently certify submissions, and owns platform/security incidents.

Legal and safeguarding owners must approve or replace that allocation in club
contracts and public wording. If Courtly reviews evidence or advertises a coach
as “verified,” define exactly what was checked, who checked it, when it expires,
what monitoring occurs, and what the claim does not mean. Until then, do not use
that claim.

## WSH and activity-safety record

For every operating venue/activity, identify the employer, occupier, principal,
contractor, self-employed party, and emergency decision maker. Each party must
determine its own duties; contract allocation does not automatically remove a
statutory duty.

Minimum record:

- routine and non-routine hazards: court condition, slips/trips, weather/heat
  and lightning, equipment, collisions/projectiles, manual handling, travel,
  lone work, remote/online sessions, child pickup/release, medical needs, and
  misconduct or violence;
- risk evaluation and hierarchy-of-control measures, responsible implementer,
  residual risk, approval, communication, and next review;
- venue inspection and allocation, capacity/ratio, participant readiness,
  equipment checks, hydration/rest, first-aid/AED access, emergency contacts,
  evacuation/shelter, supervision and handover;
- coach/staff instruction, competency, induction, and exercise evidence;
- stop-work authority and a method to notify participants/guardians without
  relying solely on child chat; and
- incident/near-miss capture, immediate care, scene preservation, escalation,
  insurer/venue notice, and a documented MOM reportability decision.

Review the risk assessment at the approved interval and after an incident,
near-miss, material venue/equipment/activity change, or evidence that controls
are ineffective. Scheduling a Class or storing venue rules is not the risk
assessment.

## Coach status changes

Suspend bookability while an expiry, allegation, restriction, insurance lapse,
or safety concern is unresolved. Preserve the minimum evidence and audit trail;
do not delete historical bookings. Assign future sessions safely, notify only
people who need to know, avoid prejudgment, manage conflicts of interest, and
document reinstatement or removal approval. An app-level roster deactivation is
one control, not the entire employment, safeguarding, participant-contact, or
regulatory process.
